# UFC opponent-adjusted wrestling × striking × submission risks — corrected research

**Updated October 10, 2026. RESEARCH-ONLY.** This report supersedes the initial [filtered-history V1 workflow](https://github.com/maxxw71/ufc-feed/actions/runs/38099283506). The authoritative corrected reconstruction is [the full-history V2 run](https://github.com/maxxw71/ufc-feed/actions/runs/38099588916). No live U1–U12 method or wager was changed.

## CRITICAL data-quality discovery and fix

The historical `new_category_discovery/prefight_favorite_features.csv` was generated by [run_new_category_discovery.sh](https://github.com/maxxw71/ufc-feed/blob/main/scripts/run_new_category_discovery.sh), which computes fighter statistics from the **price-and-reach-filtered** `ufc_reach_method_analysis/reach_market_sample.csv` instead of the entire UFCStats fight history. The filtered source excludes substantial prior UFC fights and therefore understates counts and changes averages. Inspecting **3,279** wager rows before October 10, 2026 showed:

| Historical prefight field | Rows differing vs. full-date-ordered UFCStats reconstruction |
|---|---:|
| Favorite's prior UFC bouts | **2,237** |
| Opponent's prior UFC bouts | **2,260** |
| Favorite TD attempts per 15 | **2,157** |
| Favorite control per 15 | **2,234** |
| Opponent TD attempts per 15 | **2,177** |
| Opponent significant strikes landed per minute | **2,260** |
| Opponent significant striking differential | **2,259** |

This means preliminary high-ROI interaction subsets mixing v6 style fields with newly reconstructed fight counts were **not internally consistent**. They must **not** be cited as valid method backtests.

For **full-history V2**, the analysis reconstructs both fighters' historical takedown attempts and lands, defense opportunities, completed defense, control, striking offense/defense, KO and submission outcomes strictly **before** each event date using the complete UFCStats raw archive. Other fields (date, favorite, opponent, age, height, reach, reconstructed moneylines) are carried from the historical research table. It does **not** overwrite the live scanner or the existing dataset; this is an isolated corrected research representation. More source-identity and odds validation is still necessary.

## Corrected historical groups

Criteria: favorite >=4 years younger, >=2 prior UFC fights for **both** fighters, favorite >=2.5 TD attempts and >=0.5 control min per 15, opponent <=3 TD attempts and >=2.5 significant strikes landed per minute. Shorter group adds both a height and reach deficit.

| Group | N | W–L | Historical win % | Historical ROI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Younger wrestling favorite vs striking opponent | **222** | **166–56** | **74.77%** | **+7.33%** |
| Exclude opponent high striking danger | **191** | **148–43** | **77.49%** | **+10.89%** |
| Exclude opponent submission collision | **194** | **147–47** | **75.77%** | **+8.43%** |
| Exclude TD-conversion × strong-defense collision | **213** | **160–53** | **75.12%** | **+7.21%** |
| Exclude **all three** flagged risk categories | **161** | **127–34** | **78.88%** | **+11.90%** |
| Younger wrestler shorter and with less reach | **59** | **48–11** | **81.36%** | **+19.41%** |
| Shorter group excluding all three categories | **38** | **32–6** | **84.21%** | **+22.04%** |
| Shorter >=2in and reach deficit >=4in | **9** | **5–4** | **55.56%** | **−12.77%** |

**The earlier 48–6, +30% ROI shorter-wrestler finding does not survive a complete historical profile rebuild.** It had been selected from biased prefight features. Likewise, the two-match perfect extreme-gap sample is not reliable evidence of a Camilo-like effect.

### A possible specific loss mechanism

In the 222-fight corrected cohort, opponents with **pre-fight >=5 significant strikes landed/min and >=+0.5 strike differential/min** correspond to **31** fights: the younger wrestling favorites went **18–13**, **−14.66% ROI**. The remaining **191** are **148–43, +10.89% ROI**.

For 2010–2023 discovery, baseline **134–49, +5.08% ROI (183)** became **121–38, +8.97% (159)** after excluding that striking-risk profile. But in **2025–26**, that exclusion retained only **13** of **14** fights, and the result was **11–2, +18.09% ROI**, versus all **12–2, +20.87%**. This is **not prospective confirmation**.

Submission collision (favorite prior submission loss and opponent submission wins, including sourced but incompletely verified regional histories): the **28 flagged** matches were **19–9, −0.31% ROI**, compared with the unflagged **194, +8.43%**. However the 2024 and 2025–26 flagged observations happened to be profitable, so an unconditional veto is not yet justified.

The three-factor exclusion increases the corrected group's in-sample ROI from **+7.33% to +11.90%**, but removes **39 winners and 22 losers**. Despite better net ROI per wager, selection and data concerns prevent live promotion.

### Opponent-adjusted TD defense

For every prior fight, estimate takedowns allowed using how often the attacker previously completed takedowns, with shrinkage toward 40%; compare expected against realized takedowns for that defender. Require 12+ such prior attempts across 2+ opposing fighter histories for a research-grade adjusted score. This reconstruction uses **only prior bouts** to set each past opponent's expectation.

- **142/222** fights have sufficient opponent-adjusted history (108–34, +9.87% ROI); 80 lack this quality floor.
- In the short-wrestler group, **34/59** have the quality floor (29–5, +30.55% ROI).
- Opponents with shrinkage-adjusted TDD >=68% and 12+ prior attempts faced: **19** shorter-wrestler matches, **18–1, +41.27% ROI**; **0** in 2025–26. This counterintuitive result is *not* a new selection method.
- Better-than-expected adjusted TDD: **16–0**, +55.24% in the shorter group, **0** in 2025–26. Massive selection/small-sample uncertainty and no recent validation; do not use.

## Limitations and decision

- Historical profits and betting prices are **retrospective reconstructed prices**, not verified live fills; fees/slippage/timing not simulated.
- Data-source reconstruction of older UFC fights is better than the odds-filtered sample, but fighter identity resolution uses normalized names. It still needs exact-ID identity audit and spot verification.
- Historical subgroup filters are **post-hoc hypotheses**, and the 2024+ results have been viewed in earlier research. Therefore *not* an untouched future holdout.
- Regional evidence only covers DWCS archive fighters and a small number of independently checked histories. Missing source coverage cannot be equated with zero submission danger.
- Several subgroup sizes are tiny; do not infer 100% future win probabilities from 16/16 or 2/2.

**Decision: ZERO newly promoted methods and ZERO new global live risk vetoes.** The corrected evidence makes strong opposing striking output and documented submission collisions valuable **shadow features** to track, but does not yet justify changing the production bet catalog.

**Next:** reconcile fighter IDs and authoritative profile vintages, re-run methods on full UFCStats prefight profiles, perform opponent-strength/market-matched comparisons, and collect truly prospective frozen observations before promoting any veto.
